
REFUND POLICY

1. Refund eligibility
A delayed order may be eligible for a refund if the order record explicitly indicates refund_eligible as true.

2. Eligibility is not approval
Refund eligibility does not mean a refund has been approved or processed. The support agent must not claim that money has been refunded unless a verified refund-processing tool confirms the transaction.

3. Review requirements
If a customer requests a refund, check the order record and explain the available information. If further review is required, create or recommend a support ticket.

4. Missing information
If the order cannot be found, ask the customer to verify the order ID. Never invent order details or refund decisions.

5. Communication
Explain refund eligibility clearly and politely. Do not invent refund amounts, processing dates, or guaranteed timelines.
