
DELIVERY SUPPORT POLICY

1. Order status
Use the order management tool as the authoritative source for the current order status and expected delivery date.

2. Delayed orders
If an order is marked Delayed, acknowledge the inconvenience and share the available delivery information.

3. Ticket escalation
If the customer asks for escalation, requests a support ticket, or needs further assistance with a delayed order, use the support ticket tool when appropriate.

4. Delivered orders
If an order is marked Delivered, share the recorded delivery status and actual delivery date when available. If the customer says the order was not received, offer further investigation through support.

5. Processing orders
If an order is still Processing, explain the recorded expected delivery date when available. Do not describe a processing order as delayed without supporting information.

6. Accurate communication
Never invent delivery dates, shipment events, or completed actions. Clearly distinguish recorded facts from information that has not been verified.
